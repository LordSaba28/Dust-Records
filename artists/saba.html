Artist / Producer · Dust Records

$ABA

Bio

$ABA is a genre-blurring artist and producer whose work sits at the intersection of hip-hop, Afro-fusion and alternative R&B. Signed to Dust Records in 2019, he has become one of the label’s flagship voices — known for cinematic production, introspective lyricism and a refusal to be boxed in.

His breakout project “Dust & Gold” earned critical acclaim across the continent and diaspora, while subsequent singles have racked up multi-million streams and festival slots from Afro Nation to SXSW. Off-stage, $ABA remains deeply involved in the creative process, often producing for fellow Dust roster artists.
