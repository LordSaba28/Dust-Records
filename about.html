Our Story

Built on culture.
Driven by vision.

Dust Records began as a project given to IT students by our IT provider to build, with a simple goal...

Great music deserves a home that respects both the craft and the culture that birthed it. What started as a collective of producers and songwriters has grown into a global independent force and has since scaled from then.

Today we operate across West Africa, Europe, Asia, South and North America. Still guided by the same principle... Independent sound, distinct identities, global reach.
